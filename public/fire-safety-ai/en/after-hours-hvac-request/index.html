After-hours HVAC request form

Use this checklist and blank form to request planned heating, ventilation or air conditioning outside normal hours in a commercial building. The tenant provides the area and local times, and the building team confirms what it can schedule.

When to use this form

Use it to extend service for planned evening, weekend or holiday occupancy. For a room that is already scheduled for service and has weak or failed heating or cooling, use the building’s maintenance request process. For an emergency, follow the building’s established procedures.

Check the tenant handbook or ask building management for standard hours, advance notice, minimum run times, any fees and how to make or cancel a request. These rules can vary by site and day.

What to include in the request

  • Who is requesting service. Give the tenant organization, the authorized contact and a work contact for changes.
  • The area to serve. Identify the building, floor, suite or room. Include the HVAC zone if it is known.
  • The requested window. Give the date and local start and end times. For a request crossing midnight, include the end date. For a recurring request, list the dates or the recurrence and its end date.
  • The service and any cost approval. State what service is needed and include the billing reference or approval the building requires, if applicable.

How to confirm and schedule service

  1. The property or facilities team verifies the authorized contact, served area, requested times and site rules. It asks for missing information or proposes a different window when needed.
  2. Authorized staff approve or decline the request. The reply gives the request reference, approved area and local time window, and any applicable charges or conditions. Pending requests retain an owner and a follow-up time.
  3. Facilities staff record the approved window in the building’s existing scheduling system. Changes or cancellations go through the same site process. The next shift can use the request reference to check the latest status.
