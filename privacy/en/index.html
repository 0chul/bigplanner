Privacy Policy

This policy takes effect when published on this website on 24 November 2026, Korea time.

Bigplanner Partners processes general inquiries, partnership proposals and fire safety demo requests submitted through this website as described below.

During follow-up, we may process receipt times, status, consultation notes and related project materials.

Retention and deletion

These maximum periods apply to requests received after this policy takes effect.

  • We keep general inquiries and partnership proposals for up to one year from the date we first receive each request.
  • We keep original demo requests for up to 90 days from first receipt. If you separately request a general consultation and there is a basis to process it, the retention limit for the consultation copy is one year from the original demo request. The transfer date does not restart that period.

This policy does not extend retention periods for existing records retroactively. Our policy requires prompt destruction once the purpose has been fulfilled or the retention limit reached. Electronic records must be destroyed using methods that prevent recovery or reconstruction. Administrators can delete individual general and partnership inquiries in the management interface. If a law requires us to keep particular information, we retain it separately for the required period.

Overseas transfer and processing in Singapore

Destination, timing, method and data
When you submit any of the three forms, your browser sends the required fields, optional fields you complete and submission details for that form to our Supabase production database in Singapore over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Staff also use this database when they save follow-up records.
Recipient and contact
Supabase Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). Contact its privacy team at privacy@supabase.io.
Recipient’s purpose and period of use
The processor operates the database and stores records on our instructions so we can respond to inquiries, review partnership proposals and arrange demos. Our retention limits and earlier deletion rules apply to individual submissions. Supabase’s standard processing agreement provides for processing during the service agreement, or until we request earlier deletion through the service. The provider states that managed backups are stored in the selected region for a period determined by the plan.
Support and logs
Supabase states that it processes operational logs in the European Union and that staff in other countries may access relevant information when needed for technical support or security response. Supplier’s data location guidance · Standard processing agreement
If you do not wish to transfer your information
If you leave a form unsubmitted, its answers are not sent and we do not receive the online request. For an existing request, you may contact the privacy officer below to request access, deletion or restriction of processing. The three forms do not currently obtain separate consent to overseas transfer. The partnership and demo forms ask about collection and use. Publication of this policy does not constitute separate consent to overseas transfer. We are checking whether any legal basis other than separate consent applies to the overseas processing and storage of each form’s data.
