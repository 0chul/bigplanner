Before the outgoing shift leaves

  1. Use one ticket ID for the request. Record when it came in, in site-local time, and identify the building, floor and room.
  2. Summarise what the tenant reported and record any checks or actions already completed.
  3. Identify the person or team responsible. Add the current status, the next action and a due time or check-in time.
  4. Review open items with the incoming shift and confirm who will follow up.

For emergencies, follow the building’s established contacts and procedures.
