From tenant question to shift handover

  • Answer routine questions

    The chatbot uses your building’s operating guide to answer questions about visitor parking and other services covered in the manual.

  • Record and assign a request

    For issues such as weak cooling, AI records the location and details. A supervisor reviews the request and assigns someone to follow up.

  • Hand over with the same record

    Incoming staff can see what was reported, the guidance given and who was assigned. Authorised staff confirm schedules and approvals.
