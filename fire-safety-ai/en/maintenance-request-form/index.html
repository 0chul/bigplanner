Tenant maintenance request form for commercial buildings

A free template for reporting routine HVAC, lighting or plumbing issues in a commercial building. Record the exact location, what you noticed and when staff can check it.

Blank maintenance request form

Download or print this form, fill it in and send it through your building’s approved maintenance channel. For site access, give the available time and escort arrangements.

For a fire or immediate hazard, follow the building’s emergency procedures and use its designated emergency contacts.
